01 — About

Hardware, wired for people.

I'm Saniya, a 20-year-old 2nd year B.Tech Electrical Engineering student at G H Raisoni College of Engineering, Nagpur, originally from Gadchiroli, Maharashtra.

My projects live at the intersection of sensors and impact — Arduino builds from two hackathons, and now a minor project built specifically around women's safety. I'm drawn to problems where a signal has to turn into a real, timely response.

I bring teamwork, clear communication and steady problem-solving to every build — the parts of engineering that don't show up in a datasheet.
